## Boundaries and manual review

- Tesla removed at the user's request: all three model groups, seven variants
  and its rebate audit entry. The remaining additions are four brands,
  28 model groups and 66 priced variants.
- GWM ORA 5 HEV is register-interest only, no published Malaysian price/trim
  table at https://www.gwm.com.my/en/models/ora-5-hev. Not priced or invented.
- Mazda CX-5 2.2D High remains in website/brochure but has no row in the current
  linked price PDF. MS Limited Edition has a brochure but no verified current
  OTR row. Both need distributor confirmation; do not derive prices from web
  "Price From" or another trim's fees.
- Mazda2, Mazda6 and CX-3 are absent from the current vehicles directory;
  older pages/navigation are not sufficient proof of current new-car availability.
- Mazda CKD PDFs show a separate accessories-installed amount below the OTR
  row. Base Body Price uses the explicitly labelled OTR row; separate packages
  and premium paint are disclosed, not silently folded into another trim.
- GWM RM3000 is an existing-owner loyalty rebate, not universal.
  No exact unconditional October cash rebate was
  established for the added trims, so rebates remain blank rather than claiming
  no promotion exists. Existing seven-brand rebate records are unchanged.
- Current linked older-model/year PDFs do not guarantee stock availability.
- No backend/schema/auth/notification or unrelated vehicle changes are needed.
